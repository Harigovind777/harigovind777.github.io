WildID — endangered species identification

A Flutter app that identifies CITES-protected marine species from a photo, backed by an ensemble of three PyTorch classifiers. Built for field use, so it degrades gracefully: when the network drops it falls back to a bundled on-device model instead of failing.

  • 66CITES-protected species
  • 98.47%reported top-1 accuracy
  • 50–400msinference per prediction
  • 3models in the ensemble

EfficientNet-B0 as the primary classifier, with ResNet-50 and MobileNet-V3 combined by weighted voting for robustness. Flask serves the models; the app caches for offline use.

Thesis · Private repository

Bone scaffold property prediction & inverse design

Predicting how biopolymer bone scaffolds behave mechanically, then running the model backwards to propose a scaffold worth printing. A voxel FEM sweep fused with scarce real data by multi-fidelity co-kriging.

  • 747geometries simulated
  • +0.242accuracy inflation I found
  • 266/725designs survive constraints
  • 10scaffold architectures

The finding I'm proudest of is a negative one: random 5-fold validation claimed 0.826 accuracy, but grouping the splits by publication dropped it to 0.584 — barely past the 0.555 majority-class baseline. Under a fair leave-one-architecture-out test the classical Gibson–Ashby law (R² = 0.733) beat my own learned surrogate (0.555). So the physics shipped and the surrogate didn't.
